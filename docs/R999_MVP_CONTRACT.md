# Easy Erf R999 MVP Contract

## Commercial MVP

The immediate Easy Erf product is an **Early Access Easy Erf Property Investigation for R999 once-off**.

It is not a subscription product and it is not an AI concept-generation product.

## Customer promise

A customer identifies a South African property and explains what they want to know or do. Easy Erf combines:

- available official and public-source evidence
- customer-supplied documents and property information
- structured Easy Erf research and deterministic calculations
- AI-assisted document interpretation and investigation support where appropriate
- human review before delivery

The primary deliverable is a human-reviewed Easy Erf report saved with the customer's investigation.

## Report decision structure

The R999 investigation should answer, in plain language:

1. **Property Truth** - what Easy Erf can substantiate about the property and the evidence behind it.
2. **Property Potential** - what the evidence suggests may be possible, clearly separated from approvals or guaranteed rights.
3. **Deal Killers / Key Risks** - material issues that could change the customer's decision.
4. **Conflicts** - where evidence sources disagree or do not line up.
5. **Unknowns** - important questions that remain unresolved.
6. **Next Steps** - the shortest practical actions to improve confidence or move the deal forward.

The report should also preserve useful evidence previews, provenance, calculations and any accepted site/build-area work.

## Guided Investigation contract

Guided Investigation is the normal customer journey and is stateful across the app.

The same property conclusion must not need to be entered separately on different pages. Identity, zoning, evidence status, strategy choices, Site Potential/build-area state and report state should be shared views over one property file.

Resume should reopen the first meaningful incomplete step, not restart the investigation.

## Evidence rules

- Never present an assumption as verified.
- Never present a general municipal scheme as property-specific zoning proof.
- Never present a paid provider report as the certified title deed.
- Never present an approximate build envelope as municipal approval.
- Keep the original SG diagram or uploaded source file and the Easy Erf interpretation/annotation together.
- Missing evidence remains visible instead of being filled with invented certainty.

## Site Potential MVP scope

For the R999 MVP, Site Potential focuses on:

- current site state
- parcel geometry
- street frontage
- approximate buildable area / build envelope
- planning inputs and assumptions
- uploaded site photographs, topography and plans
- accepted building-area output reused in Guided Investigation and the report

The three-independent-AI-concept generation workflow is outside the R999 MVP and should not be required or promoted in the active customer journey.

## Human review operating model

Early Access may remain partially manual. That is intentional.

Easy Erf should automate repeatable evidence gathering, extraction, calculations and report preparation while human review closes the important confidence gaps. The human-review workflow should teach the product what customers value and which research steps are worth automating next.

## Follow-on services

When the R999 investigation identifies a real gap, Easy Erf may offer or refer appropriate follow-on work such as:

- title/deeds verification
- SG/cadastral assistance
- zoning confirmation
- town planner
- land surveyor
- architect
- conveyancer/attorney
- engineer
- builder
- valuation / market evidence
- deeper financial feasibility

These are downstream of the investigation, not prerequisites for making the first product useful.

## MVP acceptance property

Erf 1570 remains the gold-standard real property used to prove the end-to-end experience.

## Build priority

When architecture work, data migration or secondary features compete with this contract, prioritize the shortest safe path to a trustworthy R999 investigation and human-reviewed report.
